# What data we need

Twelve months minimum, twenty-four preferred. CSV or Excel exports are fine; we map your column names.

| Data | Usually from | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Line-level sales: timestamp, receipt, product, quantity, price, discount, loyalty ID, medical or adult-use | POS | everything |
| Product catalog: category, size, cost, vendor | POS or inventory | margin, reorder |
| Current packages/lots: packaged date, quantity, lab status | Seed-to-sale export | freshness, reorder |
| Open purchase orders | Buyer | reorder |
| Promotion calendar | Marketing | promo P&L, forecast |
| Daily marketing spend by channel | Marketing, invoices | channel returns |
| Hourly schedule or punches | Scheduling or payroll | staffing |
| Annual operating expenses by category | Accounting | 280E |

Nothing leaves your control without a signed pilot agreement.
